Did You Know?

Important Blood Facts

O− is the Universal Donor

O negative red cells can be given to patients of any blood type in emergencies when there is no time for blood typing.

AB+ is the Universal Recipient

People with AB positive blood type can receive red blood cells from all 8 blood types — the most flexible recipient.

Blood Lasts Only 42 Days

Red blood cells have a shelf life of just 42 days. Platelets last only 5 days. Constant donations are critical to maintain supply.

O+ is Most Common Globally

Approximately 38% of people worldwide have O positive blood, making it the most frequently needed and donated type.

AB− is the Rarest

Only about 1% of the population has AB negative blood. Donors with this type are incredibly precious to our network.

1 Donation = 3 Lives

Each whole blood donation is separated into red cells, platelets, and plasma — each component helping a different patient.

See a Critical Type? Be the Hero.

If your blood type is showing critical levels, your donation today can literally save someone's life before tonight.
