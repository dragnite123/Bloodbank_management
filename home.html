How It Works

Simple, safe, and fast — donate in under an hour and save up to three lives.

1

Register Online

Create your donor profile in minutes. Tell us your blood type and nearest center.

2

Schedule Visit

Pick a time that suits you. Walk-ins are also welcome at any of our 60+ centers.

3

Health Screening

A quick check of hemoglobin, pulse, and blood pressure. Takes just 5 minutes.

4

Donate & Save

The actual donation takes only 8–10 minutes. Refresh and you're done — a hero!

Why It Matters
Benefits of Donating

Save Up to 3 Lives

Your single donation is separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets — each helping a different patient.

Free Health Check

Every donation includes a complimentary screening of blood pressure, hemoglobin, cholesterol, and more.

Reduces Heart Disease Risk

Regular donation reduces iron buildup in the blood, lowering risk of heart attacks and cardiovascular disease.

Stimulates New Blood Cells

After donation, your body replenishes lost blood, promoting production of fresh, healthy blood cells.

Donor Recognition

Earn badges, certificates, and priority blood access for family members as you reach donation milestones.

Join a Community

Become part of a network of 12,000+ life-savers. Attend donor events and connect with a cause bigger than yourself.
