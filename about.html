Our Purpose
Mission & Vision

Everything we do is guided by one purpose: ensuring no patient dies due to lack of safe blood.

Our Mission

To provide safe, adequate, and affordable blood and blood components to every patient in need — regardless of their economic status, location, or urgency — by maintaining a robust donor network and state-of-the-art processing infrastructure across India.

Our Vision

A future where zero lives are lost due to blood shortage. We envision a fully self-sufficient national blood supply system driven by voluntary, non-remunerated donation, powered by technology, and accessible to every Indian citizen within 60 minutes of need.

Our Journey
25+ Years of Saving Lives

From a single centre in Delhi to a nationwide network — our story.

1998

Founded in New Delhi

LifeServe Blood Bank opens its first centre in Delhi with a team of 12 medical professionals and a mission to eliminate blood shortage in the capital.

2004

WHO Certification & National Expansion

Received World Health Organization certification for blood safety standards. Expanded to Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata — 10 centres now operational.

2010

Digital Donor Portal Launched

Launched India's first online blood donor registration and request system, reducing average match time from 4 hours to under 45 minutes.

2016

Mobile Blood Collection Units

Deployed 30 mobile blood collection vans across rural India, reaching communities with no access to fixed blood bank facilities.

2020

COVID-19 Emergency Response

Coordinated plasma donation drives during the pandemic, supplying over 8,000 units of convalescent plasma to hospitals across 15 states.

2024

AI-Powered Blood Matching System

Introduced AI-driven compatibility matching and predictive inventory management — cutting emergency supply time to under 30 minutes nationally.

What We Stand For
Our Core Values
01

Safety Above All

Every unit of blood undergoes 8 mandatory safety screenings before release. We never compromise on donor or patient safety.

02

Equity & Access

Blood is a human right. We serve every patient regardless of their ability to pay, location, or background.

03

Transparency

Real-time stock levels, open pricing, and full traceability from donor to recipient — complete accountability always.

04

Innovation

From AI matching to mobile collection units, we continuously invest in technology to eliminate inefficiencies that cost lives.

05

Compassion

Every interaction with donors, patients, and families is treated with empathy, urgency, and deep human care.

06

Community

Our 12,000+ donors aren't customers — they're family. We celebrate, support, and grow together as a life-saving community.
