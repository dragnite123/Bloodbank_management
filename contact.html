Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about donations, requests, and our services.

How do I make an emergency blood request?
Call our 24/7 emergency line at 1800-LIFESERVE immediately. Our team will match available stock and arrange the transfer to your hospital within 30 minutes in most cities. You can also use the Find Blood page to locate the nearest centre with available stock.
Who is eligible to donate blood?
You can donate if you are aged 18–65, weigh at least 45 kg, have haemoglobin ≥12.5 g/dL, and have no recent illness, travel to malaria-endemic areas, or certain medications. A full eligibility checklist is available on our Donation FAQs page.
How often can I donate blood?
Whole blood can be donated once every 56 days (about 6 times per year). Platelets can be donated every 7 days, up to 24 times per year. Plasma can be donated every 28 days.
Is there a cost to receive blood?
LifeServe charges only a nominal processing and screening fee to cover testing costs. For BPL cardholders, Ayushman Bharat beneficiaries, and patients referred by our partner NGOs, blood is provided free of charge.
Can I organise a blood donation camp at my office or college?
Yes! We regularly partner with corporates, colleges, NGOs, and RWAs to organise donation camps. Fill the contact form above and select "Blood Camp / Drive Organisation" as your subject, or email us at donate@lifeserve.in.
How long does a blood donation take?
The entire process takes about 45–60 minutes including registration, health screening, and refreshments. The actual blood draw is only 8–10 minutes.
